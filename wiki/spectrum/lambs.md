# Support for LAMBS
When LAMBS is loaded whenever AI units use the lambs radio information sharing feature to share information from one AI squad to another, the squad will broadcast a spectrum signal that players can detect and listen to. 

The sound you can listen into will not be accurate to what the squad are doing or what kind of information they are sharing, but as a flavour to allow players to detect when AI squads are sending information to other AI squads. 

It can be disabled in CBA settings if you prefer to not have the AI radio calls show up as signals
